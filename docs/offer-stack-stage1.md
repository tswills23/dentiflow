## The constraints this is built around

- **Budget:** $500 to start. This buys a *signal*, not a customer machine.
- **Time:** Founder works a full-time job 6am–2pm PST. Sales/onboarding calls happen **after 2pm PST and weekends only.**
- **Visibility:** Founder stays invisible. Ads run under the Dentiflow brand. No founder name or face anywhere public. (See [[project-sourceclub-visibility-constraint]].)
- **Proof:** One case study (Village/32 Dental, presented publicly as an arms-length client — see [[project-32dental-stakeholders]]).
- **Delivery:** One person, part-time. Only **recall, speed-to-lead, and the dashboard** are built today. Reviews, no-show, and AI voice are still being built.

The core idea: **sell only what's built and proven (recall), keep it simple enough for one person to deliver, and use a free trial to beat the trust problem.**

---

## Main Offer — Dormant-Patient Reactivation

This is what the ads sell. One module. One price.

> **"Free 7-day test: we text your dormant patients and book them back on the schedule. If it works, it's $500/mo to keep it running. If it doesn't book anyone, you pay nothing."**

**The three parts:**

1. **Free 7-day reactivation test** (attraction). Free gets ~9x the opt-ins of a paid offer. On a $500 budget, every click has to count.
2. **$500/mo continuity** (the actual money). Done-for-you dormant-patient reactivation, running every month. This is the product.
3. **Performance guarantee** (risk reversal). "If it doesn't book patients, you don't pay." This is what makes a stranger say yes to a faceless brand with one case study.

**What the practice has to do:** hand over a dormant-patient export from their PMS and approve the message copy. That's it. We run everything.

## The Funnel (built around "calls after 2pm only")

The ad does **not** book a call — most opt-ins land while the founder is at his day job. Instead:

**Ad → case-study landing page → self-serve free-test signup form → founder qualifies → evening/weekend onboarding call.**

- The form catches leads all day while he's at work.
- He batches the qualifying + onboarding calls after 2pm and on weekends.
- Dentists are often busy during clinical hours anyway, so evening calls are a fit, not a flaw.

The case study (now scrubbed of the family relationship) becomes the **landing page** — its job changed from "warm-referral asset" to "paid-traffic conversion asset."

---

## Qualification Friction (protect limited delivery time)

Free offers can drown you if you can't deliver them all. Add friction on the signup form so only real prospects get through:

- Must be a **PPO dental practice**
- Must have at least **~300 dormant patients** (smaller lists won't show a clear result)
- Must be the **decision-maker / owner**
- Must be willing to do a **20-minute onboarding call** and provide a patient export

Friction is the answer to free-offer overload — not switching to a paid offer.

## Upsell — The Full System (earned, not pitched)

The full revenue system is the **ascension path**, not a day-one pitch. Upsells flop when you offer them before the customer has felt the next problem.

**The sequence that works:**

1. Recall runs → books dormant patients → practice sees real money come back.
2. Now they believe us. A new problem surfaces — "patients are coming back, but a third no-show," or "they come in but we're not getting reviews."
3. *That* is when the next module gets offered — as the fix for the problem they're now feeling.

Each module is its own upsell, added as the practice hits the next leak. The "full system" is the destination they grow into.

**Deliver only what's built.** Today the real upsell ladder is **recall → speed-to-lead → dashboard.** Reviews, no-show, and AI voice join the ladder as they ship. Do not sell a module that can't be turned on yet.

**Rollover mechanic:** credit what the practice has already paid for recall toward the bigger system. Makes the jump feel nearly free and converts far more.

## $500 expectation-setting

Be clear-eyed. $500 in dental B2B ads ≈ a few thousand impressions → maybe 5–15 opt-ins → 1–3 qualified → maybe **1 paying practice** if the funnel is good. That outcome is a **win**: it proves a stranger (not family, not a friend) will pay. Scaling spend comes *after* that proof, not before.

---

## Blockers to clear BEFORE spending ad money

1. **A2P / SMS sending.** Twilio A2P is listed as *pending*. Without it we may not be able to text a *new* practice's list at volume — which gates whether the free test is deliverable to anyone beyond Village. **Confirm A2P status first.**
2. **Employment clause.** Confirm SourceClub's agreement has no "outside activities" / non-compete clause that bars a dental side-business. This trumps everything.
