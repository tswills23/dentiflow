## Channel 1 — Warm referrals (Scott / Dr. Phillip → their dentist contacts)

**Goal:** 5–10 personal referrals each. ~15–30 total warm intros.
**Format:** text message or DM. Personal. Not promotional-feeling.
**Frequency:** one batch this week, follow up in 2 weeks if no response.

### Template for Scott to send

> Hey [first name] — wanted to share something we did at 32 Dental. Trevor (my son, engineer) built a recall system for the practice. We just finished a 14-day A/B test — 11 dormant patients booked from his approach vs 0 from the standard recall every other vendor sends. Real data, real production.
>
> He's looking for 3 more practices to run free 14-day tests with this quarter. No charge, no contract. Thought of you. Case study walkthrough is here: [URL]
>
> Worth a quick 15-min call with him?

### Template for Dr. Phillip to send

> Hey [first name] — we ran a recall test at Village over the last few weeks with Trevor's company. Got 11 dormant patients back on the schedule in 14 days. He's putting together a small group of practices to test this with for free this quarter. Thought you should know about it — full case study here: [URL]

### Template for Shane to send (in case strategy changes later)

> Hey [first name] — we just published a case study from the recall pilot we ran at the 32 Dental locations. 11 dormant patients booked from Trevor's 3-voice sequence vs 0 from the standard recall most vendors send. 14-day test, real production data. He's running free tests for a few more practices this quarter — thought you'd want to see it. [URL]

### Key principles for warm referral copy

- **Don't sell.** It's a referral, not a pitch. The case study sells.
- **Concrete numbers in the first sentence.** "11 vs 0" stops the scroll.
- **Specific ask: 15-min call.** Vague asks ("would you be interested?") get vague responses.
- **Single link only.** One URL. No PDF attachment. No deck.
- **No emoji, no hype words.** Operators don't trust emoji from other operators.

## Channel 2 — LinkedIn announcement post (Trevor's account)

**Goal:** generate inbound DMs from PPO practice owners and group operators.
**Frequency:** one launch post this week. Then 2 follow-up posts over the next 3 weeks pulling specific angles from the same data.

### Launch post (Day 1)

> 11 bookings vs 0.
>
> Just finished a 14-day A/B test on dormant dental patients at a 3-location PPO group.
>
> 400 patients. Randomly split into two groups.
>
> Group A got our 3-voice SMS sequence (Day 0, Day 1, Day 3).
> Group B got what every other dental vendor sends: a single message with a discount offer.
>
> Group A: 48 booking-link clicks (24%). 11 confirmed bookings (5.5%).
> Group B: 0 clicks. 0 bookings.
>
> For context — industry-typical dormant-to-booked rate is 1–3%. Group A hit 5.5%.
> Industry-typical recall click rate is 2–5%. Group A hit 24%.
>
> Just published the full case study: real anonymized conversation threads, the side-by-side templates, the funnel data, and a video walkthrough from one of the practice's co-founders (former CFO + CEO of Familia Dental, 50 locations).
>
> [URL]
>
> If you run a PPO practice or a group, the next 14-day test can be on your dormant list. Free. Comment "test" or DM me.

### Follow-up post #1 (Day 8 — the "why single-message recall doesn't work" angle)

> 199 patients got a single SMS with 30% off their treatment plan.
>
> Zero clicked the link.
> Zero booked.
> 4 opted out.
>
> This is the recall message every other dental vendor in the space sends. It's the default in Weave, NexHealth, RevenueWell. And it doesn't work on dormant lists.
>
> Here's why → [URL to case study]
>
> We tested it head-to-head against a 3-voice sequence. The 3-voice approach got 11 bookings from a near-identical 201 patients. 5x the industry-typical reactivation rate.
>
> If you're paying for recall SMS and not getting dormant patients back, this is probably why.

### Follow-up post #2 (Day 15 — the operator/DSO angle)

> Scott Wills was CFO and then CEO of Familia Dental — 50 locations across the Midwest. He spent years trying to build automated recall at DSO scale and never got it working.
>
> He left Familia to co-found 32 Dental — three PPO locations in Elk Grove Village, Illinois.
>
> Last month his son's company (mine) ran a 14-day recall test at his practice.
>
> 11 dormant patients came back in 14 days.
>
> Scott's on the case study explaining why this works when his Familia attempts didn't.
>
> If you run a group, this is worth 9 minutes: [URL]

### LinkedIn post principles

- **Hook in the first line.** "11 bookings vs 0" stops scroll. "We did a recall test" doesn't.
- **No buzzwords.** No "transform," "revolutionize," "game-changing."
- **Specific numbers > round numbers.** "23.9%" reads as real. "About 25%" reads as marketing.
- **CTA in the post AND a soft re-CTA at the end.** "Comment 'test'" gets you DMs to start a conversation.
- **One link only.** Multiple links tank LinkedIn reach.

## Channel 3 — Cold outreach (cold email + cold LinkedIn DM)

**Goal:** test message-market fit. ~100 sends, ~3% engaged (3 conversations).
**Target:** PPO practice owners + group operations leads. Filter by: US, dental, 3+ employees, "PPO" or "general dentistry" in profile/bio.
**Frequency:** 25/day for 4 days. Don't blast — pace.

### Cold email (subject + body)

**Subject options (test these in batches of 25):**
- `5.5% dormant patient reactivation in 14 days (real test, real practice)`
- `Why every dental recall vendor's default approach got 0 bookings`
- `[First Name], 11 vs 0 — quick recall test from a PPO group`

**Body:**

> Hi [first name],
>
> Quick one. We just finished a 14-day A/B test on dormant patient recall at a 3-location PPO group in Illinois.
>
> 11 dormant patients booked from our 3-voice SMS sequence. 0 from the industry-default single-message offer most vendors send.
>
> 5.5% dormant-to-booked rate (industry typical is 1–3%). 24% link-click rate (industry typical is 2–5%).
>
> We're taking on 3 more practices this quarter for free 14-day tests. No setup fee, no contract — you keep every booking.
>
> Case study + 9-min walkthrough: [URL]
>
> Worth a 15-min call?
>
> Trevor Wills
> Founder, Dentiflow

### Cold LinkedIn DM (shorter)

> [first name] — quick one. Tested our recall system on a 3-location PPO group last month. 11 dormant patients booked vs 0 with the industry-default single-message approach.
>
> 5.5% reactivation in 14 days. Real data, real practice (my co-founder is former CFO of Familia Dental).
>
> Taking on 3 more practices this quarter for free tests. Want to see the case study? [URL]

### Cold outreach principles

- **Subject line tests in batches of 25.** Measure open rates. Kill what doesn't open.
- **One CTA.** "15-min call" — no other ask in the message.
- **Reference the source of credibility.** "Former CFO of Familia" handles the "who are you" objection upfront.
- **Personalize the first line if possible.** "Saw your post about [X]" or "Saw your practice in [Y]" — 2x reply rate vs no personalization.
- **Send hours: Tue–Thu, 8–10am local time.** Highest open rates.
