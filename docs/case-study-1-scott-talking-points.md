# Case Study #1 — Scott Wills On-Camera Talking Points

**Total runtime:** 2–3 minutes on camera (split into 2 clips: cold open ~45 sec, close ~1:15)
**Recording:** simple webcam or phone, good lighting, quiet room. No script visible on camera.
**Wardrobe:** business casual. Solid color shirt. Not white.

---

## Clip 1 — Cold Open (target ~45 seconds)

This opens the case study. Sets credibility before the viewer sees a single number.

**Suggested delivery (use your own words — these are beats to hit, not a script to memorize):**

> *"I'm Scott Wills, co-founder of 32 Dental — three PPO locations in Elk Grove Village, Illinois.*
>
> *Before this I was CFO and then CEO of Familia Dental, a 50-location DSO across the Midwest. While I was there, I spent a lot of time trying to crack patient recall — getting dormant patients to come back. We had the budget. We had the team. We never got it working at scale.*
>
> *When my son's company offered to test their version of this at our practice, we said yes. What I'm about to show you is what happened over 14 days."*

**Must-hit beats (any order, your words):**
1. Your name and current role: co-founder of 32 Dental, 3 PPO locations
2. Your prior role: CFO → CEO of Familia Dental, 50 locations
3. You tried to build recall automation at Familia and couldn't crack it at scale
4. You said yes to testing your son's version at your practice
5. Set up the viewer for what's coming: "here's what happened over 14 days"

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Don't claim a Dentiflow title or role — you're a 32 Dental co-founder, not Dentiflow team
- ❌ Don't quote a specific revenue number — that comes later in the case study from a different source
- ❌ Don't say "Dentiflow is the best" or anything sounds-like-an-ad — let the data speak
- ❌ Don't promise specific outcomes for the viewer's practice

**Tone:** Direct. Slightly understated. You're an operator talking to other operators. Not a pitchman.

---

## Clip 2 — Close (target ~1:15)

This wraps the case study and sets up the CTA.

**Suggested delivery:**

> *"I want to be straight with you about why this matters.*
>
> *At Familia I watched practice owners burn money on marketing — Google Ads, billboards, mailers — to bring in new patients while they had hundreds of dormant patients already in the chart who would come back for free if anyone bothered to reach out the right way. Every PPO practice has this problem. Most of them don't know it.*
>
> *What you just saw is the difference between the way every vendor in this space handles recall and the way it should be handled. A 5x improvement isn't because Dentiflow has better SMS technology than Weave. It's because the system was built by people who actually thought about how patients reply to texts.*
>
> *If you run a practice — or a group, or a DSO — and you want to see if this works on your dormant list, the next 14-day test could be yours. There's no risk and no setup cost to find out. Talk to Trevor."*

**Must-hit beats (any order, your words):**
1. Most practices spend on marketing while ignoring dormant patients already in their chart — that's the problem
2. What the viewer just saw is the difference between standard recall and how it should be done
3. The 5x improvement isn't technology — it's that the system was actually thought through
4. Direct CTA: if you want to test this on your dormant list, the next pilot can be yours
5. No risk / no setup cost framing (matches the landing page CTA)
6. Point them to Trevor

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Don't reference specific competitor names if you can avoid it (Weave is fine since the data shows the industry default — but don't make it a competitor takedown)
- ❌ Don't make claims about how it'll work for the viewer's specific practice
- ❌ Don't say "guaranteed" anything
- ❌ Don't quote dollar amounts you didn't verify

**Tone:** Slight authority lean — this is the "trust me, I've seen what doesn't work" moment. Confident but not arrogant.

## What to do BEFORE recording

1. Read the case study script: [case-study-1-script.md](case-study-1-script.md)

4. Frame the shot: head and shoulders, eyes slightly above center of frame, plain background

1. Send raw video files to Trevor (no editing needed)
2. Trevor will cut and integrate into the final case study
3. If you want a second take on either clip, do it — first takes are usually rough

## If a viewer asks "but how do I know that revenue number is real?"

Standard response when this comes up on a sales call:

> *"I'm the co-founder of the practice the test was run at. I stood behind the number we published. If you'd like to hop on a 15-min call with me to verify, I'm happy to do that."*

That reference-call offer is way stronger than any screenshot. Don't volunteer it — but have it ready if asked.
