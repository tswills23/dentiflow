## D. WHY YOU'RE STUCK

You've already tried to fix this. Probably three times.

**Attempt one: the postcards.** You sent them. Some tiny number of people came back. You couldn't tell which ones, so you couldn't tell if it paid. You stopped.

**Attempt two: the software you already own.** Weave, RevenueWell, Lighthouse360, Solutionreach — every group has one. It sends the reminder. It's a $99/month checkbox and it is genuinely fine at what it does. But it sends *from* a no-reply number *to* a patient with a question. Nobody's home on either end.

**Attempt three: the call list.** You had someone print it. They made forty calls the first day, twenty the second, and by Thursday the phone was ringing and a patient was at the desk and the list went in a drawer. Not because your team is bad. Because a call list is the first thing that loses when the day gets busy — every single time.

Here's the root cause underneath all three.

**Sending was never the problem.**

Your patients got the postcard. They saw the text. The message arrived.

They just had a question nobody answered:

> *What is this going to cost me?*
> *Does my insurance still work there?*
> *I can't deal with scheduling right now, when could I even come in?*

A one-way message cannot answer a question. So the patient does the only rational thing available to them — nothing.

And then you look at the flat result and conclude *"reactivation doesn't work for us."*

It's not that it doesn't work. It's that **nobody in this category has ever sold you the part that comes after the send.**

## E. THE NEW MECHANISM — LEDGER-TO-CHAIR™

Three stages. This is the entire thing.

### Stage 1 — Ledger Sweep

We pull every past-due patient out of your practice management software and score them on four things: months since last visit, remaining insurance benefits, diagnosed treatment sitting unscheduled, and historical production.

Your list stops being a blob of 1,300 names and becomes a **ranked queue**.

And we blend every wave — roughly **60% recently-overdue, 40% deep-overdue**, same recipe every time. That's deliberate. It's what stops us from burning your best patients in week one and watching the rate collapse by month four.

### Stage 2 — The Conversation Layer

The AI opens a real two-way text thread. Not a blast. Not a no-reply.

When the patient writes back — and they do — it answers the three things that actually stop a lapsed patient from rebooking: **what it costs, whether their insurance still works, and when they can come in.**

It handles the reply. It handles the follow-up question. It handles the reschedule. It reads like your best front-desk person on her best day, and it never gets interrupted by the phone.

### Stage 3 — Chair Lock

The patient picks a time from the **reactivation blocks you set aside** — and it lands on your schedule.

Every booking gets attributed back: which patient, which thread, which dollar, on a live dashboard you can open any time.

### The proof, in one paragraph

At a 7-location PPO group in Elk Grove Village, Illinois, we took **657 patients** off their past-due list — about **17% of it**.

**145 clicked the booking link. That's 22%, against an industry-typical 2 to 5.**

**32 booked. About $9,600 in production.**
