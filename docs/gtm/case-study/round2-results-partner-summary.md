# Village Dental — Recall Round 2 Results

**Prepared for:** 32 Dental partners
**Practice:** Village Dental (Elk Grove Village, PPO)
**Campaign:** 3-voice recall sequence (Day 0 / Day 1 / Day 3)
**Launched:** 2026-06-03 · **Data through:** 2026-06-11 (Dentrix Ascend cross-reference)

---

## The headline

> **~21 dormant patients booked from 457 texts — roughly $4,600 in projected hygiene production, for about $5 in SMS cost.**

These are patients who had received **zero** prior outreach from us — the untouched Village dormant list.

---

## The funnel

| Stage | Count | Rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dormant patients texted | 457 | — |
| ‒ unique phones (households) | 330 | — |
| Clicked the booking link | 76 patients | **~23% of households** |
| **Booked an appointment (attributable)** | **~21** | **~4.6% of texted** |
| Opted out | 16 | 3.5% |

Industry-typical recall link-click is 2–5% and dormant-to-booked is 1–3%. Round 2 ran several times above both.

---

## How we count bookings (honest tiers)

Most bookings happen **outside our texting loop** — the patient clicks the link and books online, or just calls the front desk. Our system only "sees" a booking when the patient replies to confirm. So we cross-reference the texted list against the Dentrix Ascend schedule. Three confidence tiers:

| Tier | What it means | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| **A — System-confirmed** | Patient replied to our text to confirm | **11** |
| **B — Clicked, then booked** | Clicked our link, appointment now on the schedule | **14** |
| **C — Booked, no click** | On the schedule, but we can't prove our text caused it | 24 |
| | **Total texted patients with an appointment on the books** | **49** |

**The number we stand behind is ~21** = Tier A + the clean, future-dated Tier B bookings. We exclude same-day/pre-existing visits and the soft Tier C (can't prove attribution). The full 49-on-the-books is upside, not the claim.

---

## Revenue

- **~$4,620 projected** — 21 bookings × $220 (Round 1's practice-verified per-hygiene-visit average).
- **Not yet front-desk confirmed.** This becomes a hard number once Village's front desk returns the production export for these patients.
- SMS send cost across the whole round: **~$5.**

---

## What changed in the last week

Tracked between the 6/6 and 6/11 schedule pulls: booking **count held flat at 49**, but the bookings **firmed up** — appointments that were "Unconfirmed" became **Confirmed or Complete** (e.g. Peter Pawlak and Don Schanaberger's visits are now complete). The bookings aren't evaporating; they're showing up to the chair.

---

## Bottom line

One Village dormant list. One week of automated texts that read like a real front-desk message — no discount blast. ~21 patients back on the schedule, ~$4,600 in projected production, for the cost of a coffee. Front-desk revenue confirmation is the only number still pending.
